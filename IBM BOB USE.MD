# Technology Used – IBM Bob

IBM Bob was used as an AI-powered development assistant throughout the UDAAN project, from researching the problem statement and identifying suitable solutions to planning, implementing, and improving the application.

**1. Problem Research and Identification**

IBM Bob helped research existing problems faced by beginners, independent builders, and early-stage startups when converting ideas into working projects. It supported the exploration of existing solutions, identification of gaps in current workflows, and analysis of the challenges involved in planning, developing, testing, and launching different types of projects.

**2. Solution Discovery and Planning**

Based on the identified challenges, IBM Bob helped explore possible solutions and refine UDAAN's core concept: an AI-powered platform that guides users from an initial idea to practical execution. It assisted in structuring requirements, defining essential features, organizing development priorities, and planning workflows for different project types, including software, hardware, physical products, and services.

**3. Application Design and Development**

IBM Bob supported development activities by helping analyze the existing codebase, plan frontend and backend integration, identify implementation requirements, and develop application features. It was used to structure the development workflow for modules such as Overview, My Projects, Flight Plan, and Market Radar. It also helped formulate implementation requirements for database persistence, API integration, task management, and interactive roadmap visualizations.

**4. AI and Research Workflow Design**

IBM Bob helped plan how UDAAN could combine web research tools, including Brave Search and Tavily, with Google's Gemini API. This workflow is designed to gather competitor information, public pricing, customer complaints, and market opportunities, then use AI to generate actionable roadmaps and validation experiments supported by source links.

**5. Testing and Improvement**

IBM Bob supported the identification of implementation gaps, planning of test cases, and refinement of usability requirements. Particular attention was given to maintaining consistent application behavior, providing clear progress feedback, handling errors, preserving user changes, and connecting frontend interactions to actual backend functionality.

**Conclusion**

IBM Bob served as a development and problem-solving assistant, helping connect research, solution planning, technical design, and implementation. Its use supported a more structured development process and helped shape UDAAN into a practical platform focused on turning ideas into actionable, testable projects.
